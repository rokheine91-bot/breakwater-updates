# Tsunami Defense 0.5.10

Water 1.8: benchmarks and speed-ups. This is the last release of the water phase.

- Faster:
  - The zone's water surface is smoothed once per frame on the graphics card, instead of for every point of the drawn surface.
  - The sea takes fewer, larger steps. Each step may now cover 0.45 of a cell instead of 0.35, and all water checks still pass.
  - The zone's pressure uses 24 passes instead of 30.
  - Foam is lighter: every fifth landing drop leaves a fleck (was every third), and flecks last 2.5 s (was 3 s).
- Repeatable runs fixed. The sea's repeatable-run test failed now and then: a reset recorded after a frame's work sometimes reached the graphics card in the wrong order. Resets now happen at the start of a frame, and 6 runs out of 6 now come out identical.
- F12 bug reports now include the time each part takes (sea, 3D zone, spray) and the particle counts.
- Benchmarks on a GTX 1080 Ti, whole level program (the biggest wave is 3 m):

| Sea bed | Average fps | Worst frame |
|---|---|---|
| Rock coast | 67 | 24 ms |
| Reef shelf | 66 | 22 ms |
| Sandy bay | 64 | 22 ms |
| Long gentle shelf | 63 | 29 ms |
| Clay cliff | 59 | 25 ms |

- The 60 fps target is met on average, but not at the moment the wave hits the house: frames take 22 to 29 ms there, about 35 to 45 fps. The 3D zone is the biggest single cost then, and Z turns it off.
- All water checks pass after the speed-ups:
  - run-up within 1 to 8 % of the Synolakis lab law
  - dam break within 1 to 4 % of the exact solution
  - Green's law within 0 to 9 %
  - a lake stays still
  - the total water holds, spray included
  - the spray exchange is exact
  - the pour tool adds exactly what it should, and the ground brush makes no wave
