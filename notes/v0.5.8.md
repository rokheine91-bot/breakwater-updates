# Tsunami Defense 0.5.8

Water 1.6: the water look.

- One water surface: inside the 3D zone the surface now comes from the 3D water. Each 15 cm column's top cell counts as filled as far as its particles go, and neighbouring columns are smoothed together. It blends into the sea over a metre at the zone's sea-side edge.
- Particles are only drawn where water flies above the surface: splashes, drops, curling crests. The water under the surface is drawn as the surface.
- Colour runs from turquoise shallows through mid blue to deep blue, with a light rim where you look across the water at a low angle. The colour comes in flat bands to fit the pixel look.
- Foam (white) only shows on fast water (over 5.5 m/s) and very thin water, no longer on all running water.
- Smaller waves throw spray too: a breaking front now needs to be less steep and slower to throw spray.
- Not yet:
  - foam that drifts with the water (now it is drawn where the water is fast)
  - a proper water edge against the house walls
- Speed: drawing the zone surface costs about 1 ms more. During the impact the game runs at about 43 to 50 fps on a GTX 1080 Ti. That gets fixed in the benchmarks phase.
