## Tsunami Defense 3D 0.6.1 - water 2.0 polish, bigger diorama

**Bigger diorama**
- 96 x 28.8 m instead of 60 x 24 m (60 % longer, 20 % wider). The new land behind the house rises gently and rolls, so a flood runs out and stops on its own.
- The waves are exactly as before (the wave maker still measures from the old level's edge).
- The starting camera sits a bit further out.

**Water physics**
- No more invisible wall behind the house: the 3D water around the house used to end in a solid wall, and the flood bounced back off it as a white wall. Its land side is open now, and so is the diorama's far edge.
- Long runs with the house repeat exactly (the bricks' contact search depended on thread timing; this has been there since 0.5.10). New long test checks it.

**Water look**
- Splashes are drawn as one smooth water surface (screen-space fluid) instead of separate balls: glassy where thick, white and foamy at thin edges and where the water flies fast.
- Splash water that falls back shrinks into the surface instead of popping; slow lumps no longer lie on the water.
- Foam is milky and streaky instead of a cell network that looked like boiling; far foam no longer flickers as white grain.
- The 3D water's surface no longer twitches ("bubbling") where it is calm.
- Foam and shallow water no longer turn slate grey in the house's shadow.
- Steep water faces no longer show vertical stripes; the diorama's cut water side is a clean plain wall.
- The light stripe along the beach is gone.

**Known**
- Slower: about 34 fps on average on a GTX 1080 Ti with the biggest wave (was about 59 in 0.6.0), mostly the bigger sea and the new splash and foam effects. A speed pass comes next.
