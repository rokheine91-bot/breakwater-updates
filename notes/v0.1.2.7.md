# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2.7

New in 0.1.2.7 - water stability fixes:
- Fixed water "exploding" after a while (speeds running away, air holes and vertical streaks inside the water). Now the water settles calmly, also between concrete blocks.
- Fixed a rare multi-core bug that could make results differ between runs. Results are identical again on any number of cores (now tested on two scenes).
- Spray droplets fly straight instead of gaining energy; a safety limit stops any water going faster than 50 m/s.
- New test scene 4: water flowing over and between concrete blocks.
- Fixed a harmless error on the very first start (missing reports folder) that made an automatic bug report.

From 0.1.2.6: 40 m levels (closer view), sharp menus, Quit button.
From 0.1.2.5: automatic bug reports (crash, serious error, F12).
