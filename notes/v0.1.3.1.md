# Tsunami Defense 0.1.3.1

New in 0.1.3.1:
- Fixed water "draining": drawing concrete over water deleted that water. Now concrete pushes the water aside (up first, then sideways) - no water is ever lost.
- Fixed water getting crushed into thin layers under concrete: squeezed water now pushes itself back apart within a couple of seconds.
- Tsunami! button (top bar) and SPACE send a tsunami from anywhere: the game switches to the beach scene by itself. The game now starts on the beach.
- New keys: SPACE tsunami, Up/Down wave height, P pause, R reset, 1-6 scenes, Q brush shape, F1 shows all keys, F6 water look, F7 volume fix (test).

From 0.1.3.0: wave maker and beach scene (1.3 step 1).
