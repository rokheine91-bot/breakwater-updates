# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2

New:
- Water is about 5x faster: the solver now uses all CPU cores, only computes where water is, and reuses the last pressure result.
- Results are identical on every PC and with any number of cores.
- Water is one blue; each particle has a faint white outline. Press O to switch to solid blue.
- Controls are listed in a bar at the top.
- Ctrl + left mouse removes water, Ctrl + right mouse removes concrete.
- Mouse wheel changes the brush size; brush shape Square or Circle (buttons top right, or B).
- Pouring only fills empty pixels; concrete drawn into water pushes that water out.

Known limits:
- All graphics are placeholders.
- More speed work (pressure solver) continues in 1.2.
