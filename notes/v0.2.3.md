# Tsunami Defense 0.2.3

New in 0.2.3 - the destruction lab rework (first version, art as in the approved sample):
- Houses have depth layers: a real front wall (plaster on brick, bare brick, stone, timber boards or rendered blockwork) with windows, shutters, sills, lintels, a door, a stone base; a real back wall (the room side with wallpaper, furniture and windows); the cut structure in between. Hipped roofs show their tiled front slope.
- Windows and doors are holes in the front and back walls only. The hidden piers and hidden bracing are gone: the real walls do that job now.
- Front wall button (F): hide the front wall to look into the houses.
- Breaking down to the smallest size: a hard hit breaks a piece into bricks / planks / chunks, a harder one into grit (1 pixel). Loose bits that land hard break further.
- Rubble: grit and small bits that come to rest pile up into heaps that slide to a natural slope; falling bodies land on the heaps.
- Cracks show as dark lines along joints; plaster comes off along breaks where a part fell away (the brick shows).
- Water gets into a house through its windows and doors: glass breaks at about 0.6 m of water, a door at about 2 m, then the room fills.
- Earthquake: houses now sway at 5-16 Hz (front and back walls make them stiffer); multi-storey houses crack in a strong quake at their own frequency.
