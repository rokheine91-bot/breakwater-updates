# Tsunami Defense 0.5.2

Water 1.1 fixes after your first look.

- The water now runs on the real clock: fixed 1/60 s steps, so a fast PC no longer plays it too fast (a PC that cannot keep up plays in slow motion, same result)
- Twice the water resolution in both directions: 5 cm cells, the same as the 2D water (was 10 cm), with one surface point per cell
- The water no longer drains away at the land edge: the whole level is closed
- Red flood line removed (wet ground still shows darker)
- Smoother bores and hydraulic jumps (no saw-tooth spikes), and the ground now uses exactly the same 5 cm sea bed as the water
- Sharper picture: pixel size 2 by default (keys 1-4 still change it)
