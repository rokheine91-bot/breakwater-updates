Tsunami Defense 0.1.1 - water core (main phase 1, sub-phase 1.1)

New:
- First version of the water simulation: one water pixel = one simulated particle.
- Real water pressure (water can't be squeezed), splashing and flowing around obstacles.
- Three test scenes: 1 dam break, 2 still water (must stay calm), 3 two water columns colliding.
- Pour water with the left mouse button, draw walls with the right, erase walls with the middle button.
- Space pauses, R resets. Frame time and particle count are shown at the top.

Known limits (on purpose for this step):
- All graphics are placeholders.
- The solver runs on one CPU core; speed comes in 0.1.2.
- No tsunami, ground or destruction yet.
