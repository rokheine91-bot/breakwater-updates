# Tsunami Defense 0.2.1

New in 0.2.1 - destruction lab, the four open points from 0.2.0 fixed:
- Houses are now real cross-sections (like a doll's house): end walls, floors, roof, stilts and concrete frames are the structure, the rooms are open with the back wall, its windows and furniture behind. Windows and doors in the end walls are real glass and doors. The front and back walls (not in the slice) are counted as hidden bracing that stops each storey swaying sideways, and they can fail.
- Sea level tool (3): the sea comes in from the right and fills only what it can reach. A room stays dry until its wall, window or door gives way, then it floods. Every wet surface feels the water pressure at its depth; loose timber floats, debris is slowed by the water. The water is drawn.
- Earthquake (5): every house now sways like a damped spring at its own frequency (shown on screen). Shaking near that frequency builds up about 10 times, far from it does little. [ and ] change the shaking frequency; the view moves with the ground.
- Wrecking ball: no more fixed 20% loss - it shares its momentum with what it knocks loose and pays the real energy it takes to break each material.
- Timber and steel still bend before breaking, but a hit beyond 1.5 times their strength snaps them at once.
- Checked: 140 random houses stand on their own; brick walls and timber posts fail within 1% of the hand calculation under water; wall frequencies within 3% of the beam formula; resonance curve matches a 5% damped spring; a ball keeps 95-97% of its momentum when it smashes through.
