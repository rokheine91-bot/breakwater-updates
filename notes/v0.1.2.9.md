# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2.9

New in 0.1.2.9 - speed:
- New pressure solver (multigrid): needs 3-4 rounds instead of 20-60, and uses all CPU cores.
- Calm water takes one step per frame instead of two.
- The volume fix and foam now run on all cores.
- F5: benchmark - 30 seconds of a full level of water (about 83,000 particles). The result is shown on screen, saved, and sent automatically as a report. Please run it on both PCs!
- Scene 5: the benchmark level.

From 0.1.2.8: 32 m view, edge to edge, sharp 5x pixels on 1440p.
