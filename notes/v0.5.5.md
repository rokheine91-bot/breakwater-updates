# Tsunami Defense 0.5.5

Water 1.4, part 1: the 3D water, as a test tank. Press T in the game to open it.

- Fully 3D water: particles carry the water and a 3D grid keeps it from being squeezed. This is the method behind the impact zone, where the wave curls, pours over walls and runs up them.
- Three tanks:
  1. a dam break
  2. a dam break onto a low wall
  3. the real impact zone: 20 x 24 m, a beach from 4 m deep to 2 m above the sea, and a 2.5 m bore coming in
- Controls: R restarts, right mouse orbits, the wheel zooms, Esc goes back to the game.
- The dam break matches the Martin & Moyce (1952) lab experiment within 5 to 6 %. The water volume holds within 2 % over 6 s. The same run gives the same result every time.
- On a GTX 1080 Ti:
  - At 10 cm cells the impact zone would cost 27 to 53 ms per frame, too slow.
  - At 15 cm cells, with particles only in the top 3 cells of the water, it costs about 5.5 ms. The deep water below is carried by the grid.
  - So the zone is built at 15 cm, one of the fallbacks the plan allows. 10 cm stays an option for strong graphics cards.
- Not yet: the zone is not joined to the sea or the house. That is part 2, the next release.
