# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2.8

New in 0.1.2.8 - closer view, edge to edge, faster:
- A bit closer: levels are 32 m wide (was 40 m). On 1440p screens every game pixel is exactly 5x5 screen pixels, perfectly sharp.
- Edge to edge: the test tank is as wide as your screen (21:9 and 32:9 too), no empty side areas.
- Faster pressure solver: it now runs on all CPU cores (16 fixed bands, so results stay identical on every PC) and stops as soon as it is accurate enough.
- Worker threads react faster between the solver's many small steps.
- The log now records how long each part of the solver takes.

From 0.1.2.7: water stability fixes, multi-core fix, scene 4 (blocks).
