# Tsunami Defense 0.2.4

Destruction lab - hits behave like real hits:
- A heavy ball punches through: the roof under it gives way first, then the floor below, down to the ground floor. A 20 t ball goes all the way down in 1-4 s; a 300 kg ball stays on the roof. (1.5 t already breaks timber roofs: that is real, rafters cannot carry 15 kN.)
- No more "puff": the house no longer bursts apart the moment something touches it. Only the joints at the struck spot break first; only struck pieces turn into bits.
- A ball resting on a roof keeps pressing on it (it never falls asleep).
- The ball's size follows its weight (solid steel): 1.5 t is 36 cm across, 20 t is 1.7 m.
- The ball and the bulldozer hit the house itself (roof, floors, end walls), not the front wall standing beside their path; the front wall comes down with the house behind it.
- Grit lands on the ball and on loose pieces instead of falling through them.
- Cracks are only drawn where joints really cracked.
- Report button (F12) in the lab: saves a bug report with a picture and sends it.
Known: a big hit can slow the lab down for a second or two (the joint solver works hard while something pushes on a house); speeding that up is part of the optimisation phase.
