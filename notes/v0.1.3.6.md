# Tsunami Defense 0.1.3.6

New in 0.1.3.6 - optimization pass (laptops):
- New setting: Esc > Simulation quality > "Fast (laptops)". About 35% less work per frame: slightly less precise pressure and the volume fix every second frame. Water still keeps its volume.
- When a PC cannot keep up, the water now runs in slight slow motion instead of stuttering worse and worse.
- Laptops with 4-8 threads now use more of their cores.
- The water picture is built on all cores, without allocating memory every frame.
- Removed wasted work every frame (checksum and volume value were recalculated over all water several times per frame).

Please on the laptop: set Fast, then press F5 (benchmark) and F12 - that tells me exactly what the laptop needs next.

From 0.1.3.5: realistic light in Shaders B.
