# Tsunami Defense 0.2.2

New in 0.2.2 - new interface and a main menu:
- Main menu at start: choose TSUNAMI DEFENSE (the water game) or DESTRUCTION LAB, plus Settings and Quit.
- New look everywhere (layout B): dark panels with square corners, teal for what is selected, orange for go.
- Water game: waves card top left (program, biggest wave, status), sea bed top right, tool bar at the bottom (Water / Ground with material menu / Remove, brush shape and size), big TSUNAMI! button bottom right. Every button shows its key small next to it; all keys still work. Stats small at the bottom (can be switched off in Settings).
- Destruction lab: the same tool bar with all tools, strength, earthquake frequency, stress view, slow motion, pause, rebuild, new street; Beach and Main menu buttons.
- Menu (Esc) now has a Main menu button.
Fixes in the destruction lab:
- Floors and roofs no longer float in the air after the walls under them came down (the hidden bracing and the hidden wall beside openings now come down with their wall; joints that came apart let go).
- Doors and window frames no longer carry the house.
- Cottages no longer get a window cut into the same wall as the door.
- The earthquake finds the sway of the whole house (4 - 10 Hz), not a rattling door.
