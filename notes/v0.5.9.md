# Tsunami Defense 0.5.9

Water 1.7: water and ground tools. They work on the sea and inside the 3D zone.

- Left mouse pours water, as before. Inside the 3D zone it drops water from about 1 m above the ground.
- Shift + left mouse removes water under the cursor.
- Ctrl + left mouse raises the ground, and Ctrl + Shift + left mouse lowers it, in a 1.5 m brush. The water surface stays where it is, so the brush makes no wave. Inside the 3D zone the ground moves with it.
- D places a drain at the cursor (up to 8). Each drain takes water out every frame. Shift + D removes all drains.
- Checks:
  - pouring adds exactly the water the tool is meant to add (to 0.01 %)
  - the ground brush under still water leaves the lake still (fastest water 0.000004 m/s)
  - removing water only ever takes water away
- Not yet:
  - The mouse still finds the ground from the original sea bed. On ground you have raised or lowered, the brush and the pour can be a little off.
  - Inside the zone, brush changes show only on the drawn ground.
