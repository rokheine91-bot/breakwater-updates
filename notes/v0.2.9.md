# Tsunami Defense 0.2.9

Destruction lab - roofs:
- A hit stays where it lands: the blow of a ball only reaches the joints within 1.5-3 m of the struck spot; further away the house feels only the weight pressing on it. Before, one hit tore joints at both ends of the roof in the same moment.
- Roof tiles lie on the battens instead of being glued into one shell: a ball goes through, the tiles around the hole stay, only the ones above a broken rafter fall in.
- The ball breaks the roof tiles it passes behind, so the hole is visible from the front; the ball is drawn behind the front wall.
- The ball no longer rolls on endlessly through a wrecked house (it digs into rubble and stops), which caused late second collapses 10-20 s after the hit.
