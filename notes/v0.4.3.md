# Tsunami Defense 0.4.3 (test build: water picture on the graphics card)

- The water picture is now built on the graphics card. The water simulation itself stays on the processor, as agreed. During a 3 m tsunami the picture takes about 3 ms per frame instead of 11, and the workstation runs at 27-30 fps instead of 22.
- The picture looks the same as before.
- Includes the new buildings (0.4.1) and the bigger water steps (0.4.2).
- If the water looks wrong or missing on your PC or laptop, send an F12 report: the game falls back to the old way if the graphics card cannot do it.
