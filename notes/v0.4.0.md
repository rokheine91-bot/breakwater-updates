# Tsunami Defense 0.4.0 (test build: double resolution)

Test build of the rework - please try it and tell me how it plays on your PCs:
- Double resolution: 40 pixels per metre, the view is 48 x 27 m. The water, the ground and the destruction are all twice as fine.
- Houses are still the old drawings enlarged 2x (native 40 px/m drawings and real 1-pixel bricks come later, after the art sample).
- Includes everything from 0.2.5-0.3.0 (bug report video, collision and roof fixes, houses on the beach, wrecking ball).
- The water is about 2x faster than the first 40 px/m try, but still too slow: on the workstation 11-14 fps while a big wave runs (the game plays it in slow motion rather than skipping). Your laptop will be slower. This is what decides between real 40 px/m water and water computed at 20 px/m and drawn smooth.
- F5 benchmark and F12 bug reports work as before; an F12 report while a wave runs tells me exactly where the time goes.
