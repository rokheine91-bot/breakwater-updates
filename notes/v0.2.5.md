# Tsunami Defense 0.2.5

Bug reports now carry a video:
- F12 (and the Report button in the lab) saves the last 10 seconds of the game as a video next to the picture and the log, and sends it with the report. Works in the water game and in the destruction lab.
- The game keeps those 10 seconds in memory all the time (about 10 MB, no measurable slowdown). Only the game window is recorded.
- The video is 960 x 540 at 15 frames per second, 7-15 MB per report; sending it takes a few seconds more than before.
- A report made while another is still being sent now goes out right after it (before, it waited for the next start).
- Report folders now show the right game version.
