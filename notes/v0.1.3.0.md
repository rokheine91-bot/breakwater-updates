# Tsunami Defense 0.1.3.0

New in 0.1.3.0 - sub-phase 1.3 (the tsunami), step 1: wave maker and beach
- Scene 6 (press 6): land on the left, a sloping beach, a 6 m deep sea on the right.
- W sends a wave in from the right edge; [ and ] change its height (0.5-8 m, default 3 m).
- The wave maker works like a lab wave tank: it sits hidden just off the right edge, pushes in the exact water level and speed of an arriving solitary wave, and absorbs waves that come back.
- Note: a real tsunami is very long (a 3 m wave here is about 40 m long), so it arrives as a fast rise of the sea that floods the land - not yet a curling breaking wave. Breaking and the rushing bore come in step 3.
- Foam now stays near the surface (no more foam blobs deep under water).

From 0.1.2.10: streak fix, hitch fix, firmer water, multigrid solver, F5 benchmark.
