# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2.6

New in 0.1.2.6 - closer view:
- Levels are now 40 m wide instead of 60 m: everything is 1.5x bigger. Pixels scale exactly 3x on 1080p and 4x on 1440p screens (including 3440x1440), so they stay perfectly sharp.
- Menus and text are drawn at your screen's real resolution, sharp at any size.
- Quit button (top right and in Settings).
- The log and debug overlay (F3) now show how long drawing the water takes each frame.
- The solver can use up to 32 threads (was 16).

From 0.1.2.5: automatic bug reports (crash, serious error, F12).
From 0.1.2.4: crash fix, logger, F3 debug overlay.
From 0.1.2.3: realistic water look (O switches).
From 0.1.2.2: water keeps its volume (D switches the fix).
