# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2.3

New in 0.1.2.3 - realistic water look (first sample of sub-phase 1.7):
- Water colour by depth: light turquoise at the surface, deep blue in deep water, in a pixel-art palette with dithering.
- Light on the surface and a moving shimmer in shallow water.
- Foam: water that crashes, breaks or flies as spray turns white and fades over a few seconds. Calm water stays clean.
- Drawn by the graphics card; it never changes the physics.
- O switches the look: realistic, flat blue with outline, solid flat blue (also in Settings).

From 0.1.2.2:
- Water keeps its volume (99% after a long dam break instead of losing a quarter). D switches the fix off and on to compare.

From 0.1.2.1 / 0.1.2:
- Settings window (Esc), widescreen for any screen, water always at real speed, about 5x faster solver, brush tools.

Known limits:
- Look is a sample: no ground, wetness or reflections yet (those need sand and rock from 1.4).
- Concrete and background are still placeholders.
