# Tsunami Defense 0.4.2 (test build: faster water)

- The water takes bigger steps: the bulk of the water may move 3 cells per substep (was 1.5). During a 3 m tsunami on the beach the water solver needs about 1 substep per frame instead of 2-3, and the solver time drops from about 42 ms to 28 ms per frame on the workstation.
- Same results in the run-up, paint and determinism tests.
- Includes the new buildings from 0.4.1.
- Please try a big wave on your laptop and send an F12 report while it runs: it shows where the remaining time goes.
