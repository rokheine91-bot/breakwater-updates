# Tsunami Defense 0.1.3.5

New in 0.1.3.5 - Shaders B goes realistic:
- Light in the water now follows real physics: water absorbs red light fastest, then green, then blue. Shallow water is clear and turquoise, deeper water fades to dark blue-green, and whatever is behind the water fades out naturally with depth.
- The water's own blue-green glow is dimmer the deeper it is (less sunlight reaches down).
- Softer edge where the underwater backdrop starts.
- Choose it with F6 (or Esc > Water look > Shaders B). Shaders A is unchanged for comparison.

From 0.1.3.4: Shaders B clear water with underwater backdrop.
