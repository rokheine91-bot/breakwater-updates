## Tsunami Defense 3D 0.6.2 - waves break by their own physics

**Real breaking, no fakes**
- All faked breaking is gone (the pushed-forward wave face, painted crests and crest foam).
- The 3D water now covers the whole stretch where waves steepen and break, from deep water through the beach to past the house. Waves steepen, spill, plunge and run up as bores by the simulation itself.
- Cells stay 15 cm for now. 10 cm works, but its hand-over from the sea still throws back about 30 % of a wave; it comes in a later update once that is found.

**The diorama's cut side shows the real water**
- The long sides of the diorama now show the 3D water in profile (like the 2D game did): the breaking front, the bore, the splash against the house and the backwash, as the physics does them.

**Calmer, more natural surface**
- The top surface no longer bubbles: it is drawn from the smooth sea surface that follows the 3D water.
- Fast water is no longer automatically white: foam comes from breaking fronts, impacts and flying water, and is carried by the flow in streaks.
- No more curtains, spikes and jagged teeth along the diorama's sides.

**Removed**
- The drain tool (D). D now only moves the camera.

**Tests**
- Lab tests unchanged; the sea-to-3D hand-over reflects 3.6-3.8 % (as in 0.6.1); long runs with the house repeat exactly.

**Known**
- About 29-33 fps on average on a GTX 1080 Ti with the biggest wave (34 in 0.6.1).
- A few small holes can show in the side profile.
