# Tsunami Defense 0.1.2.4

New in 0.1.2.4 - crash fix, logger and debug tools:
- Fixed the crash in 0.1.2.3 on Windows: the water module could not hand its picture to the game, every frame.
- Logger: everything is written to %APPDATA%\TsunamiDefense\logs (system info, settings, every action, performance every 5 s, every engine error). Lines are saved at once, so they survive a crash. The last 10 sessions are kept.
- If the game did not close normally last time, it tells you on the next start and keeps that log.
- F3: debug overlay with frame and solver time graph, solver phase timings, water health checks, memory and the latest log lines.
- F12: bug report - saves a screenshot, this and the previous session's log and the current state to %APPDATA%\TsunamiDefense\reports.
- The launcher now writes install.log next to the game.

From 0.1.2.3: realistic water look (depth colour, light, foam), O switches the look.
From 0.1.2.2: water keeps its volume (D switches the fix).
From 0.1.2.1 / 0.1.2: settings (Esc), widescreen, real-time speed, faster solver, brush tools.
