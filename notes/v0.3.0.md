# Tsunami Defense 0.3.0

Houses on the beach:
- The beach levels now have houses from the destruction lab on their land (as many as fit; wide screens get more). The tsunami really hits them: they block the water, the water's push and current break them (the same joints, cracks and debris as in the lab), windows burst at about 0.6 m of water against them and doors at about 2 m, and the rooms behind fill up.
- Wrecking ball tool (N switches its weight: 0.3, 1.5, 5, 20 t): press where it should start, release to drop it; drag to throw it.
- F shows or hides the front walls of the houses (the cut shows the rooms and the water inside).
Not yet: debris that floats or is carried by the water (next step); for now what falls into the sea is gone. A tsunami button in the destruction lab comes after that.
